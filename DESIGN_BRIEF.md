## World Statement

HydraCut lives in a forensic graph operations room where every dependency path is handled like evidence on a dark drafting table.

## Accent Color

Signal vermilion `#F15A3C`. It marks the active cut, selected path, and decisive action without borrowing the purple and blue language of generic AI products.

## Background Treatment

Warm graphite surfaces built from `#0C0E0F`, a nearly invisible drafting grid, restrained paper grain, and low-contrast graph linework. Panels feel like evidence trays layered on a technical workbench. There are no gradient blobs, star fields, or decorative glow clouds.

## Typography Domain

Developer infrastructure and security audit. Use IBM Plex Sans Condensed for display hierarchy, IBM Plex Sans for interface copy, and IBM Plex Mono for hashes, coordinates, queries, timestamps, and proof evidence. Do not use Inter, Geist, Arial, or an undifferentiated system stack as the product voice.

## Rejection List

- No purple-led palette or purple and blue AI gradients.
- No neon cyberpunk treatment, glowing blobs, or decorative glassmorphism.
- No generic Inter, Geist, Arial, or default system-font identity.
- No decorative graph nodes that are disconnected from real evidence.
- No excessive pills, oversized radii, floating card soup, or arbitrary shadows.
- No stock imagery, generic AI illustrations, meaningless charts, or fabricated evidence visuals.
- No motion without informational purpose, and no motion that ignores reduced-motion preferences.
- No route may look unfinished, lose workflow context, hide a Forge-approved feature, or imply universal safety.

## Required Design Depth

- Apply one coherent production-grade system to every application route and state.
- Account for the full flow: import, baseline exposure, incident triage, impact inspection, proposed-fix evaluation, coverage planning, combined verification, and immutable receipt.
- Cross-check every user-facing row in `FEATURE-OBSERVABLES.md` against a reachable and wired surface.
- Make the real graph traversal the visual hero while retaining the pair table as canonical evidence.
- Specify text hierarchy, data formatting, diagrams, containers, borders, alignment, spacing, responsive behavior, focus states, loading, empty, partial, error, and verified states.
- Use animations only for meaningful graph traversal, job progress, state changes, panel disclosure, and route continuity.
- Capture before and after screenshots for every route at desktop and mobile.

## Selected Design Tier

Tier A. Full-depth Design Forge treatment is required across all routes and the complete approved Forge scope.
