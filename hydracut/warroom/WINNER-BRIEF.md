## Thesis

1. **WINNING ARGUMENT:** HydraCut is a proof-carrying AppSec incident command system. CampaignRadius uses native HydraDB many-source traversal to prove authentic portfolio dependency exposure; HydraCut discovers or accepts real proposed fixes, reconstructs their complete resolved graphs, and proves the chosen portfolio plan with a second native traversal.
2. **EVIDENCE:** The frozen real corpus contains three public repositories, 1,742 package instances, 1,253 unique exact versions, 2,939 dependency edges, 105 exact versions with current OSV matches, three baseline minimist exposure pairs, one production-scope pair, zero selected-incident pairs after three authentic proposed fixes, and six remaining pairs from two other real advisory-backed sources. Evidence source: `docs/evidence/2026-08-19-pre-forge-runtime.json` in the parent workspace.
3. **DEMO OBLIGATION:** Judges witness immutable repository and lock hashes, a live baseline `algo.MSpaths` query, an authentic proposed-fix comparison, one final combined `algo.MSpaths` proof, and the honest result that the selected incident clears while other known exposure remains.
4. **HERO FLOW:** Open the action-first incident queue, select the minimist incident, inspect affected applications and one shortest witness, review real proposed fixes and transparent coverage, verify the selected combined plan, then open the proof receipt with query bounds and provenance.
5. **INVARIANTS:** self-hosted HydraDB OSS is load-bearing; only explicit OpenCypher and native paths decide exposure; all inputs are authentic and immutable; results are bounded, reproducible, and fail-closed; no LLM, vectors, hosted-SDK-only computation, untrusted package execution, fabricated demo state, or universal safety language.
6. **DRIFT TRIPWIRES:** generic blast-radius dashboard; graph visualization as product truth; automatic fix generation; arbitrary graph cuts; exhaustive `2^N` as main story; opaque risk score; function-reachability or exploitability claims; `SAFE`, `CLEAN`, or unqualified `FIXED` language.
