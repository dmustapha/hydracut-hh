# HydraCut Forge Concerns

[C] Authenticity: no fabricated repository, advisory, dependency, path, proposed fix, count, or safety result may enter product or demo output.
[C] HydraDB irreplaceability: self-hosted HydraDB OSS, explicit OpenCypher, and native `algo.MSpaths` must compute both baseline exposure and final combined-plan verification.
[C] False-clean prevention: any incomplete advisory retrieval, graph extraction, selector cardinality, traversal bound, cursor, duplicate-pair, or final requery must refuse `VERIFIED_WITHIN_BOUNDS`.
[C] Proposed-fix authenticity: every verified fix must resolve to an immutable commit or hashed uploaded lockfile and be fully reconstructed before comparison.
[C] Demo integrity: the three-minute demo must use the frozen real corpus and display raw query bounds plus residual non-selected exposure.
[I] Operational risk: HydraDB local-file garbage collection emitted `put_opts` warnings; production robustness and restart recovery remain unverified.
[I] External dependency risk: GitHub, OSV, CISA KEV, and FIRST EPSS require explicit freshness, retry, timeout, rate-limit, and refusal contracts.
[I] Scope risk: exhaustive `2^N` enumeration must remain evidence, not the product capacity claim; planning must use bounded coverage selection plus a final combined proof.
[I] UX risk: AppSec incident command is the default; developer and leader projections must preserve the active evidence context without producing divergent truth.
[A] Polish: graph visualization supports explanation but never substitutes for the pair matrix, evidence drawer, or native query proof.
